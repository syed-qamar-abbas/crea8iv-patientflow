# Pricing - PatientFlow

PatientFlow is clinic management and patient retention software by Crea8iv Media for clinics in Pakistan.

## Starter

- Price: PKR 25,000/month
- Annual offer: PKR 150,000/year when paid in advance
- Best for: single-location clinics that want appointments, operational patient profiles, billing, staff roles, inventory, reporting and a public booking site
- Included:
  - Smart appointments
  - Operational patient profiles
  - Billing and invoices
  - Treatment packages
  - Staff roles
  - Inventory
  - Financial reports
  - Public booking site
  - White-label branding
  - Automated backups

## AppointmentFlow AI

- Price: PKR 40,000/month
- Annual offer: PKR 336,000/year when paid in advance
- Best for: growth-minded clinics that want reminders, reviews, reactivation and WhatsApp automation running 24/7
- Included:
  - Everything in Starter
  - WhatsApp Center
  - Automated appointment reminders
  - Patient recall campaigns
  - Patient reactivation campaigns
  - Review automation
  - Meta lead capture
  - Administrative AI receptionist
  - AI reply suggestions
  - Advanced analytics
